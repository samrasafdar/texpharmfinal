About Us

Discover the story behind Tex-Pharm Inc — our legacy, values, and vision for the future

25+ Years Excellence Industry Certified Global Presence Eco-Friendly

Leading the Way in Textile & Chemical Solutions

Tex-Pharm Inc is a premier provider of advanced textile testing instruments, specialized chemical processing solutions, and expert industrial consultancies, established in 2010. We combine cutting-edge technology with sustainable engineering to deliver premium-quality laboratory equipment, high-performance dyes, and specialized industrial chemicals to clients across the globe.

Our Mission

To provide innovative, sustainable, and cost-effective textile instruments and chemical solutions that empower industries to achieve operational excellence while minimizing environmental impact.

Our Vision

To be a global leader in textile instruments and chemical innovation, setting new standards for quality, sustainability, and customer satisfaction.
