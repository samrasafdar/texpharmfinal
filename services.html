Our Services

Tex-Pharm Inc provides integrated textile and chemical solutions for modern industries

Textile & Chemical Industry Solutions

Tex-Pharm Inc specializes in combining advanced textile manufacturing with high-quality chemical processing. Our operations support fabric production, dyeing, finishing, and industrial chemical supply while maintaining strict quality and environmental standards.

Textile Manufacturing

High-capacity textile production including weaving, knitting, and fabric processing with modern machinery.

Dyeing & Finishing

Advanced dyeing and finishing solutions ensuring color consistency and fabric durability for premium results.

Chemical Processing

Industrial chemical processing for textile treatments, coatings, and surface enhancements with precision.

Quality Control & Testing

In-house laboratories ensuring fabric strength, color fastness, and chemical safety for every product.

Industrial Supply & Logistics

Reliable supply chain management for raw materials, chemicals, and finished goods with real-time tracking.

Environmental Compliance

Eco-friendly processes ensuring environmental protection and regulatory compliance for sustainable growth.
